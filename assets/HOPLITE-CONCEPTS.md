# Hoplites squelettes — propositions graphiques

**Choix retenu : A · Gardien de bronze.** Les animations et leur intégration sont décrites dans [HOPLITE-ANIMATION-PROMPTS.md](HOPLITE-ANIMATION-PROMPTS.md).

Trois propositions pour remplacer visuellement les crocodiles dans le contexte grec de JumpingJack. Chaque planche présente deux poses d’intention du même personnage. Ce sont des concepts statiques, pas encore des animations de production. Aucun ennemi ni comportement du jeu n’est modifié à cette étape.

Équipement commun demandé : squelette humain, casque de hoplite, bouclier rond de hoplite (aspis), épée courte (xiphos). Rendu réaliste peint, os visibles, bronze usé, silhouettes adaptées à un jeu de plateforme de profil. Les PNG conservent leur transparence.

## A · Gardien de bronze

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: enemy character design proposal A for JumpingJack, a richly rendered ancient Greek side-scrolling platform game with realistic ivory marble temples, warm bronze and turquoise sea. Create a polished full-body character concept sheet with EXACTLY TWO large views of the SAME animated HUMAN SKELETON hoplite warrior, side by side with ample transparent margins. Left: readable three-quarter SIDE PROFILE facing RIGHT, calm guard stance. Right: same skeleton facing RIGHT taking a deliberate forward walking step, short sword held ready. Human skeleton anatomy clearly visible: skull, spine, ribs, pelvis, two bony arms, two bony legs with recognizable feet. No skin, no flesh, no blood. Wears a weathered BRONZE CORINTHIAN HOPLITE HELMET with cheek pieces and a modest dark red horsehair crest; skull jaw and teeth remain visible. Carries a ROUND CONVEX HOPLITE ASPIS SHIELD in the left hand, bronze rim and dark warm bronze face with a small embossed sun rosette, held to the side so ribcage remains readable. Holds a SHORT straight double-edged XIPHOS SWORD in the right hand, clearly distinct from a spear. Warm ivory bones, restrained dark ochre patina, realistic painterly archaeological game art, fine worn metal surfaces and crisp readable silhouette at small sprite size. Slightly stooped vigilant posture, grounded proportions, no oversized body armor obscuring skeleton. Consistent scale, equipment and lighting in both views, both feet on same imaginary baseline. Warm sunlight upper left, soft cool fill. Genuine transparent alpha=0 background everywhere outside character. No scenery, no ground plane, no ground shadow, no text, no UI, no labels, no borders. No spear, no cape, no robot, no neon aura. This is a graphic design proposal, not an animation atlas.
