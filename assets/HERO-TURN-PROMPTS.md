# Retournement du héros

Images créées avec l’outil intégré **image_gen**, à partir de `hero-actions-v3.png`. Fichiers utilisés : `hero-turn-ground.png` et `hero-turn-air.png`, chacun avec 8 poses (grille 4 × 2).

Les images sont préparées sur une cellule commune de 120 × 120 pixels natifs, préchargées sur le GPU. L’ancrage horizontal suit le pivot du corps. Le cycle est parcouru en avant ou en arrière selon la direction demandée ; il peut s’inverser sans recommencer depuis le début. Le déplacement et le saut continuent pendant le pivot, d’une durée d’environ 0,16 seconde.

## hero-turn-ground.png

Use case: identity-preserve. Input image is the EXACT superhero reference: original adult athletic man with dark hair, gold falcon half mask, dark teal fitted suit, gold falcon chest insignia, gold belt and boots/bracers, flowing crimson cape, painterly semi-realistic Egyptian platform game style. Preserve his identity, physique, costume and head scale. Create a production transparent sprite atlas EXACTLY 8 consecutive poses of a smooth 180-degree RIGHT-to-LEFT in-place turn, in STRICT GRID 4 COLUMNS x 2 ROWS, square cells, whole sheet 2:1 landscape. Order left-to-right then next row. Pose1 RIGHT side profile, pose2 slight turn toward viewer, pose3 three-quarter right, pose4 almost frontal, pose5 almost frontal toward left, pose6 three-quarter left, pose7 almost left profile, pose8 complete LEFT side profile. Real 3D yaw rotation of body/head/cape, not mirrored copies or dissolves. Same scale and stable body-root/pelvis coordinate in all cells, full silhouette inside each cell with 7% clear margin. Clean fully opaque character with genuine alpha=0 everywhere outside silhouette; no gray/white halo, glow, haze, ground, shadows, text or grid lines. Warm light from upper left. Grounded pivot: feet near the 94% cell-height baseline, head near 5%. Slightly bent athletic knees, quick foot pivot steps, torso turns first, cape swings and follows the rotation. This must work for reversing a run as well as standing; no dramatic crouching, no bouncing or changing height. Pelvis stays centered, feet remain at the same baseline. Match the standing hero's proportions from row1 of reference.
