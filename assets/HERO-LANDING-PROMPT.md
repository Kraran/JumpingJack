# Réception du héros après une chute

Création avec l’outil intégré **image_gen**, à partir de la référence `hero-actions-v3.png`. Atlas enregistré dans `assets/hero-land-v1.png`, huit poses en grille 4 × 2, préparées en cellules de 120 × 120 pixels avec ancrage (.54, .96). Réception légère pour les chutes inférieures à 160 unités ; les variantes moyenne et lourde sont décrites dans [HERO-LANDING-LEVELS-PROMPTS.md](HERO-LANDING-LEVELS-PROMPTS.md). Lecture unique en 0,24 seconde, interrompue par un saut ; aucun déclenchement en vol plané. Les miroirs sont préparés et préchargés comme les autres animations.

## Prompt exact

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: production sprite atlas for existing platform game. Reference image hero-actions-v3.png defines the SAME adult male superhero: teal fitted suit, gold falcon mask and emblem, gold bracers and boots, crimson cape, dark hair, realistic painterly style. Create EXACTLY 8 sequential frames of a gentle natural LANDING knee-bend and recovery, facing RIGHT in side profile throughout. Strict 4 COLUMN x 2 ROW square-cell grid, whole image 2:1 landscape, read left to right then next row. Frame1 feet first touch floor with legs nearly straight; frame2 knees begin bending and hips lower; frame3 both knees visibly flex about 35 degrees, torso slightly forward; frame4 deepest modest knee flex about 45 degrees, hips down but no heroic three-point crouch; frame5 begins extending; frame6 half recovered; frame7 almost upright; frame8 neutral upright matching idle reference. Show anatomical bending of legs, never simply squash body. Keep boots planted at exactly the same floor baseline 96% down EACH cell, body root center x54% of cell, no horizontal travel, consistent limb lengths and head size in all frames. Match standing sprite height about 90% of cell and transparent margin; compressed poses naturally shorter. Hands loose by sides with subtle balance movement; cape settles after impact. Entire character cape feet fit in own cell. REAL transparent alpha=0 outside silhouette, opaque character. No floor, no cast shadows, no dust, no scene, no text, no grid lines, no glow.
