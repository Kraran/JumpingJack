# Mort du héros par lame de plateforme

Asset : `hero-blade-death-v1.png`. Créé avec l’outil intégré imagegen, à partir de `hero-atlas-v2.png` comme référence de personnage. Le PNG transparent sélectionné est copié dans le projet sans modification.

Seize poses dessinées, déclinées au chargement en 64 poses par sens dans deux atlas GPU. Les gouttières de la planche suivent les hauteurs variables du personnage ; une échelle anatomique commune et un ancrage au pied sont conservés. Une petite trace de sang est préparée dans un atlas séparé. Aucun chargement, miroir ni interpolation d’images sur CPU pendant le jeu.

La séquence de 1,2 seconde commence sur la collision ascendante réelle. Après un recul de 0,16 seconde, la physique fait retomber le corps sans commande ni planage. La vie est retirée une seule fois ; résurrection ou Game Over attendent la fin. La manette vibre pendant la séquence ; la pause coupe la vibration et la reprise ne relance que sa durée restante. Les poses et la chute sont conservées dans les sauvegardes.

Aperçu : `python preview_blade_death.py --native-check`.

## Prompt imagegen

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production 2D animation sprite sheet for the SAME Egyptian superhero as the reference. Reference is character identity and rendering style only. Generate a 4 columns by 4 rows sprite sheet of SIXTEEN successive distinct poses, reading left-to-right, top-to-bottom, equally spaced identical 256x256 cells on a 1024x1024 genuinely transparent canvas. Strict side view, every pose faces RIGHT. Exact same adult hero: dark teal fitted suit, short dark wavy hair, gold eye mask, gold falcon chest emblem, gold belt, gold forearm bracers and gold shin boots, flowing dark red cape. Same realistically painted detailed 2D game art. Animation: DEATH AFTER HITTING A DOWNWARD SHARP PLATFORM BLADE FROM BELOW. Draw the HERO ONLY; the actual platform/blade is supplied by the game, so no weapon, no platform, no environment in this sheet. First row 1-4: upward jump abruptly struck at upper shoulder/head area, torso sharply recoils and bends around impact, startled pained posture, elbows jerk outward, red cape whips vertically UP. A single small dark red cut mark on the shoulder, VERY few tiny blood droplets, no gore and no detached body parts. Second row 5-8: body recoils away and drops lifelessly, head slumps forward, arms and legs becoming loose, cape begins to settle. Third row 9-12: knees give way and the limp hero collapses forward naturally onto his side, bracer hand touches ground then body rolls gently onto side, cape drapes. Fourth row 13-16: settles flat, lying motionless on his side, legs relaxed and extended, face still to the right, cape falling flat around him; last pose entirely inert. Keep the SAME body proportions and uniform anatomical scale in ALL cells: an upright hero would be 180 pixels high. Lower poses must stay physically smaller in height, NEVER magnify lying poses. Keep the hero centered horizontally around column midpoint and feet or lowest body resting point on a shared baseline y=235 within EACH cell. Allow cape to extend above head with clear margins; NO pose should cross a cell boundary. Do not add a grid, dividing lines, frame numbers, captions, text, light bursts, cyan lightning, explosions, skeletons, organs or bones. Transparent background, crisp alpha edges, all sixteen poses distinct and smoothly progressing from recoil to collapse.
