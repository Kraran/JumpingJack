# Mort du héros — dévoré par le crocodile du Nil

Images créées avec le **skill imagegen**, outil intégré, sans API externe. Asset final : `hero-crocodile-death-v2.png`. La première génération a servi de référence intermédiaire ; la révision réduit la taille du héros pour retrouver les proportions du jeu.

## Prompt initial exact

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Production transparent sprite sheet for an existing Egyptian side-view platform game. The two input images are STYLE AND CHARACTER REFERENCES ONLY, not edit targets. Create ONE new synchronized CROCODILE DEVOURS HERO death animation, 16 frames in an EXACT uniform 4 columns x 4 rows grid, read left-to-right top-to-bottom, transparent alpha background, no text or numbers or grid lines. Each cell is the SAME camera, SAME scale and SAME stationary crocodile body anchor. Strict side profile facing RIGHT in all 16 cells. Realistic painted sprites matching reference Nile crocodile: olive bronze natural scales, long curled tail, four short muscular legs, long narrow snout, natural anatomy. Hero matches reference exactly: dark teal fitted Egyptian superhero suit, GOLD falcon emblem on chest, gold mask/headband, gold bracers and boots, red cape, dark hair. Hero is human adult, about 1.2 times the crocodile's head-to-floor height, much shorter than its full nose-to-tail length. Crocodile is always fully visible tail at 3% cell width, snout near 85% width, feet on 90% cell height, body center anchored at x55%. Keep all action inside each cell with gutters, NO cell cropping. Frames 1-3 crocodile opens mouth, hero directly in front of snout recoils and raises arms; 4-6 jaws seize hero around torso, legs and cape lift off ground; 7-10 crocodile jerks head slightly up/back, hero pulled horizontally into mouth feet first, no stretching or duplicated limbs; 11-13 hero progressively disappears into jaws, only red cape remains briefly then vanishes; 14-16 mouth closes, small swallowing motion, settled crocodile alone. Clear continuous progression, smooth small changes between consecutive frames. Crocodile body and tail must NOT drift or change size. Non-graphic arcade fantasy defeat: NO blood, NO wounds, NO gore, NO dismemberment, NO explosion, NO scenery or ground shadow, no other characters. Cinematic warm lighting and detailed materials matching provided game sprites.
```

## Révision exacte

```text
Edit target image 1: the 4x4 crocodile-devouring-hero transparent sprite sheet. Supporting character reference image 2: actual hero sprites. Correct ONLY the animation scale and cell framing. Preserve the crocodiles, poses, 16-frame progression, side view facing RIGHT, 4 columns x 4 rows uniform grid, transparent background, lighting and painting style. CRUCIAL: the hero is too large. Reduce the hero's height and overall size to about 65 percent of his current size in EVERY visible-hero cell, with the feet at the same ground baseline and his torso remaining naturally at the crocodile's snout. At full upright pose the hero must be only about ONE QUARTER of the crocodile's full nose-to-tail length. Crocodile full length is about four hero heights. Smaller hero, NOT a bigger crocodile. Keep the red cape, gold boots, gold falcon chest emblem and dark teal suit. Adjust how much of the small hero is swallowed so the sequence still reads: recoil, jaws seize, lift and pull inside, cape disappears, settle. No blood, wounds, severed limbs or gore. Ensure tail tips are NOT clipped against cell boundaries: fit the whole silhouette in each cell with 3 percent gutters. All crocodiles remain exactly same size and baseline and same body root in every cell. No labels or ground or backdrop. Preserve true alpha transparency.
```

## Intégration

16 poses synchronisées du crocodile et du héros, puis 64 images précalculées par sens, dans deux atlas GPU. Le corps garde une échelle commune ; l'appui au sol est aligné à chaque cellule et les pixels isolés des gouttières sont exclus au chargement. La prise dure 0,22 seconde et rejoint la position de la mâchoire depuis la collision réelle. La séquence complète dure 1,75 seconde. Aucun sang ni démembrement.

Une vie est retirée une seule fois au contact. Les commandes sont ignorées pendant la prise et la réapparition attend la fin de l'animation ; la dernière vie mène alors au Game Over. La pause gèle le clip. Les sauvegardes conservent la phase de la mort, y compris si la dernière vie a déjà été retirée. Les anciennes sauvegardes reçoivent une séquence inactive.

`python preview_crocodile_death.py --native-check` produit la vidéo des deux sens et de la dernière vie. `python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_crocodile_death.py -v` vérifie collisions réelles, vies, pause, sauvegarde et absence de transformations d'images pendant le rendu.
